# How to Review a Revised Manuscript - Reviewer Guide

## 📍 Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Check Your Reviewer Dashboard
1. Log in as a reviewer
2. Go to **Dashboard** → **Reviewer Dashboard**
3. Look for the **"Revised Manuscripts Awaiting Your Review"** section (purple/amber banner)
4. You'll see a table with all revised manuscripts that need your re-review

### Step 2: View Revised Manuscript Details
In the revised manuscripts table, you'll see:
- **Manuscript Title** - The submission title
- **Author** - The author's name
- **Revision Type** - Minor (⚡) or Major (🔴)
- **Author's Notes** - Brief excerpt of author's revision notes
- **Due Date** - How many days you have to complete the review
- **Status Badge** - Shows if due date is approaching

### Step 3: Access the Review Form
1. Click the **"✎ Review Now"** button (purple button on the right)
2. You'll be taken to the revision review form

### Step 4: Review Form Fields
The form displays:
- **Manuscript Title and Author** - For context
- **Original Revision Request** - Why the editor requested revisions
- **Author's Revision Notes** - What the author changed
- **Download Link** - Link to download the revised manuscript file

### Step 5: Submit Your Revision Review
Complete these fields:
1. **Recommendation** - Select one:
   - ✓ Accept
   - ⚡ Minor Revisions
   - 🔴 Major Revisions
   - ✗ Reject

2. **Comments for Author** - Feedback visible to the author
   - Be constructive and specific
   - Highlight improvements where applicable

3. **Comments for Editor** - Confidential notes for editor only
   - Internal observations
   - Suggestions about the overall quality

4. **Rating** (Optional) - 1-5 star rating

5. Click **"✓ Submit Revision Review"**

### Step 6: Confirmation
- You'll see a success message
- Review will be marked as completed
- Editor will be automatically notified
- Your review appears in your "Revisions Completed" stat

---

## 🎯 Key Points to Remember

✅ **Revised Manuscript Context** - You're reviewing changes made in response to your original feedback  
✅ **Compare Against Original** - Consider if author addressed your points adequately  
✅ **Author Visibility** - Author will see your "Comments for Author" but NOT your confidential notes  
✅ **Editor Decides Final Outcome** - Your recommendation informs the editor's final decision  
✅ **Anonymity Maintained** - Author cannot identify you  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Stats

After reviewing revised manuscripts:
- **Revision Reviews** - Shows how many are pending
- **Revisions Completed** - Shows how many you've finished

---

## 🔄 What Happens Next

After you submit your revision review:
1. Editor sees your feedback
2. Other reviewers also submit their revision reviews
3. Editor makes final decision:
   - ✅ **Accept** - Manuscript is published
   - ❌ **Reject** - Manuscript is rejected
   - 🔄 **Request Further Revisions** - Author gets another revision request (cycle repeats)

---

## ⏱️ Important Dates

Look at the "Due" column in the revised manuscripts table:
- **Green text** = More than 3 days left
- **Orange text** = 3 days or less
- **Red text** = Overdue

Try to complete all revision reviews within your due date to keep the process moving!

---

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Can't see revised manuscripts?**
- Refresh your dashboard
- Check if any original reviews have been marked as "completed"
- Contact your administrator if issues persist

**Can't click "Review Now" button?**
- Make sure you're logged in as a reviewer
- The manuscript may already be reviewed by you

**Download link doesn't work?**
- Try again - may be temporary issue
- Contact administrator if persistent
